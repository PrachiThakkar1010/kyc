# Know Your Champions (KYC)

### Every champion has a story. Every story deserves to be known.

India celebrates its medals, but do we know the people behind them?

The athletes who represent our country at the Asian Games put years of hard work, discipline, sacrifices, and determination into reaching that stage. Yet, beyond the medal tally and a few headlines, many of their stories remain unknown.

**Know Your Champions (KYC)** is an initiative to change that.

Built around the Asian Games 2026, KYC aims to create awareness and recognition for India's champions by bringing their journeys closer to people. It is not just about celebrating medals; it is about recognising the individuals, their struggles, their comebacks, and everything it took to get there.

Because a medal tells us what an athlete achieved. Their story tells us what it took to achieve it.

---

## What is KYC?

KYC is a storytelling platform dedicated to Indian athletes and teams at the Asian Games 2026.

Through individual athlete profiles, team stories, and medal records, the platform brings together the achievements and journeys of the people who represented India.

### What you'll find here

- **Athlete Stories:** Discover the journeys behind India's champions, from their beginnings and turning points to setbacks, breakthroughs, and podium finishes.
- **Team Stories:** Get to know the teams that made history and the individuals who contributed to their success.
- **Champions Directory:** Explore Indian athletes and their achievements at the Asian Games 2026.
- **Medal Tally:** Follow India's medal-winning performances across sporting events.
- **Cheers:** Leave messages of appreciation and encouragement for the champions.
- **Community Feedback:** Help improve the accuracy of information and stories through feedback and corrections.

---

## Why KYC?

Recognition should go beyond the podium.

We often remember the medal but forget the journey. We celebrate the victory but rarely talk about the years of preparation, the setbacks, and the determination behind it.

KYC is a small effort to bridge that gap.

We want more people to know the names, faces, and stories of India's champions. We want their achievements to reach beyond sports enthusiasts and inspire people from all walks of life.

**Twenty medals, a hundred medals, or a thousand medals — behind every achievement is a human story worth telling.**
